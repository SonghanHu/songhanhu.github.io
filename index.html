Hi, I'm Songhan. I grew up by the sea in Qingdao, went to college at UC Irvine, and moved east for a master's in data science at Harvard. I was a teaching fellow there, where I got some practice translating long Python error messages into English.

What I do

At Bank of America, I work on the models traders use to price interest rate swaps and see how a move in rates would affect their portfolios. One question I spend a lot of time on: how do you hedge a trade when the closest thing you'd use as a hedge barely trades? I like that the answer has to make sense both on paper and on a busy morning at the desk.

Some of my favorite projects start with a question that sounds simple. At Harvard, one of those was what a piece of jade jewelry should cost. Getting a computer to look at a piece of jade was the easy part. Figuring out what the photo could actually tell you about its price was the fun part.

Outside of work

I play badminton year-round and ski in the winter. Golf has taught me how quickly a great tee shot can turn into a search party. I also play poker, where my favorite question usually comes about five minutes after the hand ends: “Wait, what would you have done there?”
